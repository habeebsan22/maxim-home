Why Maxim

Built for ambition.
Designed for life in the UK.

There are over a million Nigerians in the UK. They earn in pounds, save in naira, send money home, and spend years building productive lives here. The credit system treats them as invisible. No bureau history. No product built for the life they actually live across two economies.

Maxim is built for them. Not as an afterthought. Not as a segment inside a generic product. As the primary customer, from the first line of code.

As Maxim grows, our impact should grow with it. That is why we are committing 1% of our profits to initiatives that expand opportunity through education, healthcare, and the communities we serve.

Frequently asked questions

What is Maxim?+

Maxim is a financial app built for Africans in the UK, combining a UK bank account, a credit card that recognises your full financial history, multi-currency accounts, and rewards, all in one place.

How does Maxim assess my eligibility for credit?+

We look at your full financial profile, not just a UK bureau file. Whatever your evidence supports on day one, we match you to a card and give you a path to more as you build your history with us.

What credit card will I get?+

The card you qualify for depends on the evidence you provide during onboarding. You'll see exactly what you're eligible for after a quick identity and profile check.

Is my money secure?+

Yes. Maxim is designed with security at every layer: bank-grade encryption, biometric protection, and continuous monitoring to keep your money and personal information protected.

Do I need a UK credit history to apply?+

No. Maxim is built specifically for people the UK credit system can't yet read. A blank UK credit file is not a barrier to getting started.

Why should I choose Maxim?+

Because Maxim is built for you as the primary customer, not as an afterthought. One app for banking, credit, multi-currency, and rewards, designed for life across two economies.
